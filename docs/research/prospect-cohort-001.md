# Prospect Cohort 001 — India Paid-Pilot Validation

## Purpose

This is the first targeted outbound cohort for EXP-001. It is not a generic agency directory. Prospects are prioritized where public evidence suggests they deliver custom client software, MVPs, SaaS, or web applications and therefore are likely to encounter post-contract scope changes.

Founder-approved offer used for this cohort:

- ₹4,999 one-time guided pilot;
- one live project/example;
- up to 30 days;
- first 3 paid pilots;
- no subscription commitment during the pilot.

## Qualification rule

Before sending outreach, confirm the company still appears active and serves external software clients. Prefer contacting a founder, delivery/project leader, or public sales/business channel. Do not collect or store unnecessary personal data.

## Why these eight first

The first five are the highest priority because they combine relatively small agency size with explicit custom software/MVP work. The goal is not maximum outreach volume. It is to get five qualified conversations quickly enough to learn whether scope-change pain is frequent, commercially meaningful, and worth paying to solve.

The strongest signal is effy because its own published client material acknowledges scope creep as a recurring development-partnership issue. That makes the opening message specific rather than hypothetical.

## Outreach sequence

1. Start with the top five prospects.
2. Use one personalized observation from the company's public site; do not send generic AI-product copy.
3. Ask for a short conversation about how they handled their most recent ambiguous client request.
4. Do not lead with a product demo. Lead with the operational/commercial problem.
5. If the pain is concrete, offer the ₹4,999 pilot on one anonymized live case.
6. Record outcomes in `docs/research/customer-discovery.md`.
7. Move to prospects 6–8 if response volume is insufficient or after the first five have been contacted.

## Core outbound message structure

- Observation: show that we understand the agency delivers custom software/MVP/client projects.
- Problem question: ask how they decide whether a new request is inside the signed scope before engineers start work.
- Value proposition: ScopeDelta turns the agreed scope + new request into a cited, reviewable scope decision and change-order draft while keeping the agency in control.
- Ask: 15-minute conversation around one recent scope-change incident.
- Commercial step only after pain is confirmed: ₹4,999 guided pilot for one live project, up to 30 days.
